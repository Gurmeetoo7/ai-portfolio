BillBuddy · Completed case study

Should BillBuddy cut prices after cancellations doubled?

My recommendation: restore subscription pauses before cutting prices. I framed the pricing decision, designed the six-agent workflow and defined how its findings had to be checked.

Evidence · AI workflow · Decision
September → October

210 → 430 cancellations

The CEO proposed a 20% price cut. Would a lower price help customers who needed to stop paying for a month?

Inside one survey response

What the customer selected

What the customer described

Paraphrase: Business was slow. They tried twice to pause the subscription, then cancelled.

How I interpreted it

Retain the selected price reason and classify the written account as a failed pause. Budget pressure and a failed action can coexist. The comment leaves open whether a discount would have retained this customer.

How the investigation flowed

Three parallel analyses fed one diagnosis. Expand each stage to inspect its handoff.

1 · Tickets

Compare customer messages with support advice. Retain ticket IDs and distinguish closure from confirmed resolution.

2 · Reviews

Group stars and themes by month and version. Compare rates within each review sample.

3 · Surveys

Cross-check selected reasons with comments and tenure. Reconcile counts and retain blanks.

↓ Combined findings

4 · Test competing explanations

Compare the price hypothesis with the pause-failure explanation. Keep surveys, tickets and anonymous reviews as separate populations.

◇ Human checkpoint · Is the evidence sufficient?

I requested matched ticket and survey records in date order before moving to option costing. The records supported investigating the pause flow. The fraction of all exits caused by the defect remained unresolved. Where a connection could not be established, the attribution stayed open.

↓ After review

5 · Compare options

Assess three options, including the proposed price cut, using a common time horizon. Separate lost revenue from implementation costs.

6 · Prepare the decision pack

Prepare a leadership reply and summary, customer outreach and support guidance. Check each number against its source and denominator.

Repair the pause flow first.

All 20 October reviews describing pause failures came from version 5.2. Support advised cancellation and re-registration in 16 of 31 pause contacts. A manual pause option would give support an immediate alternative.

At an unchanged plan mix, a 20% cut requires 25% more paid account-months to preserve revenue. The survey covered 100 of 430 October leavers. Retention impact remains unmeasured; track successful pauses and subsequent exits after a repair.
